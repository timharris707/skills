## [v1.0.0] - 2026-08-19 — the always-on voice skill ships

### Added

- Initial release: the auto-firing chat-voice skill governing all prose the
  agent emits. Adapted from Lauren Tan's `unslop` in pstack
  (github.com/cursor/plugins, MIT). Owns the repo's single merged AI-tell
  catalog in `references/tell-catalog.md`: unslop's 31 patterns folded
  together with writing-for-humans' last-mile-scrub clusters, plus the
  cluster-judgment, voice-sample, and no-fabrication guardrails from that
  scrub. The body carries the short form, the self-audit, and the boundary
  map against writing-for-humans, huh, and the conduct rules.
