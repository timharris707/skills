# Changelog — fit-audit

All notable changes to the **fit-audit** skill. Unversioned while in
`skills/in-progress/`; versioning starts at promotion.

The format follows [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/).

## [Unreleased]

### Added
- **Team mode**: auditing the catalog against an incumbent stack. Adds an
  incumbent-inventory step (read the team's own skills, rules files, and
  decision records before scoring) and widens the verdict beyond ranked
  adoption: redundant-with (naming the house equivalent), conflicts-with
  (quoting the contradicted rule; evaluated on mechanics, not
  auto-disqualifying), and adapt (naming the concrete rewrite changes). The
  evidence rules govern every verdict. Surfaced by a real audit of the catalog
  for the DecisionCloud team, whose house skills and recorded decisions the
  individual-only mode had no verdicts for.
- **Initial skill** (#268): rank the catalog's promoted skills for one specific
  person, every top pick citing a real incident from their own history that the
  skill would have prevented or shortened. Evidence rules (nothing invented,
  permission before reading history), the four-step run (gather evidence,
  inventory catalog, score Fit/Benefit with S–D tiers, report with citations),
  and the ends-at-the-report boundary. Positioned ahead of setup: run the audit
  first, bind the discipline after.
